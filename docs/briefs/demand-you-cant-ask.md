# brief — demand-you-cant-ask《问卷里没有下一代产品》

## ① 想法一句话
需求分两种：当前需求只会逼出增量改良，创新瞄准的是没人说得出的潜在需求——人们不知道自己要什么，直到你把它摆在他们面前；所以创新不是回答问卷，是生成需求。

- L114-117: "We actually want to address potential demands…Potential demand is nobody knows…but once you present them, 'Oh my God, we can have this kind of solution'…we address the potential demand and to some extent, we say innovation is to generate demand."
